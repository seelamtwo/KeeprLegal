# Keepr Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 30, 2026

Keepr ("Keepr", "we", "us") is an iPhone app that saves the posts, links, and screenshots you share to it and turns them into useful notes — recipes, workouts, lessons, stock ideas, trips, contacts, and more. This policy explains what information Keepr collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

If you have questions, contact us at **[support@sysnacorpsolutions.com](mailto:support@sysnacorpsolutions.com)**.

## Summary

- You need an account to use Keepr. We store your account and the posts you save so they are available when you reinstall or switch phones.
- To build a note from a post, Keepr sends the post's link and text to our servers, which use a content-fetching service and an AI model to extract the useful parts.
- Your **Stash** (the combined list of everything extracted) is kept on your phone. It can be rebuilt from your saved posts.
- We do **not** sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
- You can export your notes at any time and delete individual posts in the app. You can ask us to delete your account and all of its data.

## Information we collect

### Account information

When you create an account we collect:

- **Email address** and a password (passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them), or
- The identifier and email provided by **Sign in with Apple** (this may be an Apple private relay address) or **Sign in with Google**.

We also store your plan (free trial or subscription), trial dates, and how many posts you have saved in the current period, so we can apply plan limits.

### Content you save

When you share something to Keepr, we store it with your account:

- The link, title, source app, text shared with the post, and text read from screenshots (text recognition runs on your phone).
- Screenshots, thumbnails, and voice notes you attach.
- Your notes, tags, collections, favorites, and reminders.
- The note Keepr extracts from the post (for example, the recipe's ingredients and steps). Video transcripts are used only while extracting and are not stored with your post.

### How shared posts are processed

To extract a note, the link and text of the post you shared (and, for image posts, the text read from the images) are sent to our servers. Our servers may:

- Retrieve the post's public caption, transcript, or top comments from the platform it came from (for example Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or Facebook) using a content-fetching service.
- Send that content to an AI model to organize it into a note.

Only the post you shared is processed. We don't access your social media accounts, messages, or feeds.

### Usage and device information

When you open the app, Keepr sends a small usage signal containing: a random install identifier created by the app, your app version and build, iOS version, device model, language/region setting, App Store country, and your plan. We use this to count active users, understand which versions are in use, and apply plan limits. It does not include the content you save.

### Diagnostics

To improve extraction quality and fix crashes, the app may send diagnostic reports when a share is processed, fails, or the app exits unexpectedly. A report can include the shared link, the platform, the extracted title and summary, the type of note produced, any error message, the app version, and the device model.

### Feedback and support

If you send feedback in the app or email us, we receive your message, the email address you provide, your app version, and your device model. Feedback is stored and emailed to our support inbox so we can reply.

### Purchases

Subscriptions are purchased and processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive the status of your subscription (for example, which plan is active and when it renews) but never your payment card or billing details.

### What we don't collect

Keepr does not collect your contacts, precise location, photos library (other than the images you choose to share), browsing history, or advertising identifier, and does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Information kept only on your phone

- **Stash**, including items you mark as done and items you remove from it.
- Text recognition on screenshots, which runs on your device.
- App preferences such as category and tag rules.

Because the Stash lives on your phone, it is deleted if you delete the app. On a new phone it is rebuilt from the posts saved to your account, but done and removed marks are not restored. You can use **Export** in the Stash to keep your own copy.

## How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Provide the app: save your posts, extract notes, sync them to your account, and restore them on a new phone.
- Manage your account, free trial, subscription, and plan limits.
- Send service emails, such as sign-up and password-reset codes and trial reminders.
- Respond to feedback and support requests.
- Measure usage, diagnose problems, and improve extraction quality.
- Protect the service against abuse and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your saved content for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

## Service providers

We share information only with service providers that help us run Keepr, and only as needed for the purposes above:

| Provider | What it does for Keepr | Information involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Hosting, database, file storage, sign-in (servers in the United States) | Account, saved posts, files, usage and diagnostics |
| OpenAI | AI model that turns a shared post into a note | Text of the post you shared (link, caption, transcript, text from images) |
| Scrape Creators | Retrieves the public caption, transcript, or comments of a post you share | The link you shared |
| Resend | Sends sign-in codes, password resets, reminders, and feedback emails | Your email address and the email content |
| Apple | Sign in with Apple and App Store subscriptions | As described in Apple's privacy policy |
| Google | Sign in with Google | As described in Google's privacy policy |

According to OpenAI's API policy, data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default and may be kept for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

## Data retention

- Your account and saved posts are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
- When you delete a post in the app, it is deleted from our servers.
- Usage and diagnostic records are kept for as long as they are useful for measuring and improving the service, and are deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.
- Feedback is kept as long as needed to resolve it.
- An account that was created but never confirmed contains no saved content; we may delete such accounts after a period of inactivity.

## Your choices and rights

- **Export:** Use Export in the Stash (everything or one category) or on any post to send a copy by email, Messages, Notes, or Files.
- **Delete posts:** Delete any post in the app.
- **Delete your account:** Email [support@sysnacorpsolutions.com](mailto:support@sysnacorpsolutions.com) from the address on your account and we will delete your account, saved posts, and files within 30 days.
- **Access or correct:** Contact us to request a copy of the information we hold about you or to correct it.
- **Subscriptions:** Manage or cancel in your iPhone's Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, or California), you may have additional rights, such as objecting to certain processing or requesting that we limit how we use your information. We honor these requests as required by law. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access controls that let each account read only its own posts and files. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

## Children

Keepr is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## International users

Keepr's servers are located in the United States. If you use Keepr from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
