# Keepr Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 1, 2026

## Key Points About Our Privacy Practices

- Keepr lets you save content you share to it — links, posts, screenshots, and text — and turns it into organized notes in categories like Recipes, Workouts, Stocks, Courses, Contacts, Travel, and Streaming.
- You need an account to use Keepr. The posts you save are stored with your account on our servers so they're available after a reinstall or on a new phone.
- Keepr only processes what you actively share to it. It has no standing access to Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp, or any other platform.
- Your **Stash** (the combined list of everything extracted from your posts) and its done/removed marks stay on your phone. Text recognition on screenshots also runs on your phone.
- We do not sell your personal data, show ads, run advertising or tracking SDKs, or collect your advertising identifier.
- You can export your notes, delete individual posts, and delete your account at any time in the app (**Settings → Account → Delete account**).

## 1. Overview & Scope

Keepr ("we," "us," or "the App") is operated by Sysna Corp Solutions ("Company"), Texas, USA. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the App, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have.

The App is available for iPhone through the Apple App Store. It lets you share content from other apps (such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, or your messaging apps) or your own screenshots, so the App can read, extract, and organize that information for you.

This Policy does not apply to the third-party apps or websites you share content from. Their own privacy policies govern how they handle your data.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Account information

To use the App you create an account with one of the following:

- **Email and password** — we collect your email address. Your password is stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see it. We email you a 6-digit code to confirm your email and to reset your password.
- **Sign in with Apple** — we receive an Apple account identifier and an email address, which may be an Apple private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email."
- **Sign in with Google** — we receive a Google account identifier and your email address.

We do not ask for your name. We also store your plan (free trial or subscription), your trial dates, and how many posts you have saved in the current period, so we can apply plan limits.

### Content you save (stored with your account)

When you share something to the App, we store it with your account on our servers:

- The link, title, source app, and any text shared with it
- Screenshots and images you share, and thumbnails of links
- Text recognized from your screenshots (recognition runs on your phone; the resulting text is stored and processed as described in Section 3)
- Your notes, tags, collections, favorites, and reminders
- The organized note ("card") the App extracts from the post — for example a recipe's ingredients and steps. Video transcripts are used only while extracting and are not stored with your post.

If more than one account signs in on the same phone, each account only sees its own posts.

### Information kept only on your phone

- **The Stash** — the combined list of facts extracted from your posts, grouped by category — and the done/removed marks you apply to it
- App preferences such as category and tag rules

The Stash is deleted if you delete the App. On a new phone it is rebuilt from the posts saved to your account, but done/removed marks are not restored. You can use **Export** to keep your own copy (see Section 8).

### Device permissions you may grant

- **Contacts** — only to add a contact that you choose to save from a post. The App writes that one contact; it does not read, upload, or store your address book.
- **Notifications** — to remind you about saved posts and before your free trial ends. These are scheduled on your phone; we do not use push notifications or collect a push token.

The App does not request access to your photo library (you pick or share specific images), camera, microphone, location, or calendar. You can change permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

### Usage information

When you open the App or your plan changes, the App sends a usage signal containing: a random install identifier created by the App, app version and build, iOS version, device model, language/region setting, App Store country, your plan, and, if you subscribe, the product, price, and currency. When you are signed in, this signal is linked to your account. It does not include the content you save.

### Diagnostic information

When our servers process a share, they record technical details linked to your account — the platform, the type of note produced, whether processing succeeded, how long it took, and the processing resources used — so we can fix problems and manage costs. These records don't include the content of your post. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may also provide us with crash reports, which don't identify you.

### Fraud prevention (free trial)

Each person and each device can use the free trial once. To enforce this we keep:

- Salted, one-way hashes of your normalized email address and of your Apple or Google account identifier, with the date your trial started. A hash cannot be turned back into your email.
- A "trial used" marker with the trial start date, stored in your iPhone's Keychain on your device.
- A "trial used" flag for your device, stored by Apple through Apple's DeviceCheck service. DeviceCheck does not give us any identifier for your device.

These records are kept after you delete your account so the trial cannot be restarted (see Section 8).

### Purchase and subscription information

Subscriptions are purchased and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card or billing details. We receive your subscription status (which plan is active), the product, price, and currency.

### Support communications

If you send feedback in the App or email us, we receive your message, the email address you provide, your app version, and your device model. Feedback is stored on our servers and emailed to our support inbox so we can reply.

### What we don't collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts list, photo library, browsing history, audio or voice recordings, payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or your device's advertising identifier, and we use no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

- **Read and organize what you share.** When you share a post, the link, any shared text, and the text recognized from your screenshots are sent to our servers. Our servers may retrieve the post's publicly available caption, transcript, top comments, and images through a content-retrieval software vendor, and then send that content (text and, for some posts, the post's public images) to OpenAI to identify what kind of information it is and produce a title, summary, and organized note. Processing can start as soon as you share, even before you open the App.
- **Recognize text in screenshots.** Text recognition runs on your phone. The recognized text is then processed as described above.
- **Save and sync your posts** to your account, and restore them when you reinstall or sign in on a new phone.
- **Take actions you ask for**, such as adding a contact or scheduling a reminder.
- **Manage your account, free trial, subscription, and plan limits**, including preventing repeat free trials.
- **Send email** through our email provider, Resend: sign-up and password-reset codes, trial reminder emails, and replies to your feedback.
- **Operate, maintain, and secure the App**, diagnose bugs, and prevent abuse.
- **Improve the App**, including the accuracy of extraction and categorization.

Authorized Company staff can view account email addresses, plans, usage counts, and feedback in an internal administration dashboard, for support and operations only. Authorized staff may also review saved content, such as shared links and extracted notes, to troubleshoot problems and improve extraction quality. Access is limited to people who need it for these purposes.

### Our service providers

| Provider | What it does for Keepr | Information involved |
|---|---|---|
| Cloud hosting and database vendor | Database, file storage, authentication, and server functions (United States) | Account, saved posts and images, extracted notes, usage, diagnostics, feedback, fraud-prevention hashes |
| OpenAI | AI model that turns a shared post into an organized note | Text of the post you shared (link, caption, transcript, comments, recognized screenshot text) and, for some posts, the post's public images |
| Content-retrieval software vendor | Retrieves the publicly available caption, transcript, comments, and images of a post you share | The link you shared |
| Resend | Sends sign-in codes, password resets, trial reminders, and feedback emails | Your email address and the email content |
| Apple | Sign in with Apple, App Store subscriptions, DeviceCheck | As described in Apple's privacy policy |
| Google | Sign in with Google | As described in Google's privacy policy |

Each provider receives only what it needs to perform its function and processes it on our behalf. According to OpenAI's API data-usage policy, data sent through its API is not used to train OpenAI's models by default and may be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.

We don't use your saved content to sell you ads, and we run no advertising or tracking SDKs in the App.

- **Legitimate interests** — to secure, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App (usage and diagnostic information), and to prevent abuse of the free trial (fraud-prevention records), provided those interests don't override your rights.
- **Legal obligations** — to comply with applicable law and lawful requests.

We may use aggregated or de-identified information that can't reasonably be linked to you to understand general usage trends; we don't attempt to re-identify it.

## 5. Content From Instagram, Meta, TikTok, YouTube & Other Platforms

**User-initiated only.** The App has no standing connection to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp, or any other platform, and does not poll, browse, or retrieve anything on its own. Content is retrieved only after — and only because — you shared that specific link or message to the App.

The App is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc., Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Google, or WhatsApp. Their names are used only to describe compatibility.

**How we get content from a link you share.** The App does not log into those platforms or access your account there. A content-retrieval software vendor we use retrieves the publicly available caption, transcript, top comments, and images associated with that specific link, which OpenAI then reads and organizes. We only process the individual link you chose to share.

**We can only see what's public.** If a post is from a private account or has restricted visibility, it cannot be retrieved, and the App can only use what you shared directly (such as a screenshot or text).

**Messaging apps.** When you share a message to the App, we only receive the specific text or image you choose to share through your device's share sheet. We have no access to your WhatsApp or other messaging accounts, contacts, or chats.

**Changes to platform access.** Because this depends on publicly available data and third-party providers, these features may change or become less reliable if a platform restricts public access or a provider changes its service.

**Your responsibilities and other people's information.** Content you save remains subject to the rights of its owners and the terms of the platform it came from; Keepr is for saving content for your own personal reference, and you're responsible for making sure your use complies with applicable law and those terms. Public posts and their top comments can include other people's names, usernames, or photos. Please avoid saving personal information about other people unless you have the right to do so. Content owners can ask us to remove content as described in our [Terms of Use](../terms-of-use/).

Providing this information is your choice. We use it only to provide the features you request, and we don't use it to make unrelated inferences about you, sell it, or use it for advertising. ## 8. Data Retention, Security & Your Rights

### Retention

- **Account and saved posts** — kept while your account is active.
- **Deleting a post** — removes it from our servers.
- **Deleting your account** (Settings → Account → Delete account) — immediately deletes your account, saved posts, images, extracted notes, feedback, and scheduled trial reminders from our servers, and removes that account's posts and Stash from your phone. Usage records are kept only in de-identified form (no longer linked to you). Fraud-prevention hashes, the Keychain marker, and the DeviceCheck flag are kept so the free trial can't be restarted. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel it in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
- **Unconfirmed accounts** — accounts that were created but never confirmed contain no saved content and may be deleted after a period of inactivity.
- **Diagnostics and usage** — kept as long as useful to operate and improve the App, then deleted or de-identified.
- **Service providers** — may keep data for limited periods under their own policies (for example, OpenAI up to 30 days), and backups age out on a rolling schedule.

### Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption at rest by our hosting provider, and access controls that let each account read only its own posts and files. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and associated data — available directly in the App
- Export your content — use **Export** in the Stash (everything, one category, or one entry) or on any post to send a plain-text copy by email, Messages, Notes, Files, or other apps
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Residents of the EEA, UK, and Switzerland have these rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR; residents of California and other U.S. states with comprehensive privacy laws have similar rights. To exercise any right, contact us (Section 11) or use the in-app options above. You can revoke device permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

**International data transfers.** Our servers, provided by our cloud hosting vendor, are located in the United States, and our service providers may process data in the United States. If you use the App from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. Where required by law, we use appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, for transfers from the EEA, UK, and Switzerland.
